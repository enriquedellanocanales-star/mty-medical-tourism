**Created**: June 10, 2026
**Status**: FINAL — Approved by operations.
**Purpose**: Single source of truth for package inclusions, recovery nights, and pricing. All procedure pages must reflect this document exactly.

## PACKAGE INCLUSIONS (all procedures)

### Package Includes
- Procedure
- Physician Fees
- Hospital Fees (when applicable)
- Transportation Coordination
- Antaris Fundidora Accommodation
- Scheduled Follow-Up Visits
- Bilingual Patient Coordination

### Package Does Not Include
- Airfare
- Companion Expenses
- Personal Purchases
- Additional Recovery Nights
- Additional Medical Services Not Originally Planned
- Additional Testing Not Originally Required

---

## ADDITIONAL RECOVERY POLICY

> "Additional recovery nights may be required if the treating physician determines that additional monitoring or recovery time is medically necessary. Additional lodging, transportation, and related services are not included in the original package price and will be billed separately."

## PAYMENT SCHEDULE

| Payment | Amount | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Reservation Deposit | 30% of package price | At booking |
| Remaining Balance | 70% of package price | At least 7 days before procedure date |

---

## CANCELLATION SCHEDULE

| Timing | Refund |
|---|---|
| Within 24 hours of deposit | 100% of deposit refunded |
| After 24-hour grace period | Deposit non-refundable |
| More than 30 days before procedure (after grace period) | Option A: Reschedule at no charge, OR Option B: 50% of deposit refunded (50% of the deposit, not 50% of the package price) |
| Less than 30 days before procedure | No refund |
| No-show without prior notice | All payments non-refundable |
